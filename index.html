How to copy coordinates

Open the map and zoom in on the position you want.

  1. Right-click the location.
  2. Click "Mark Coordinates."
  3. The coordinates automatically appear in the game's chat box.
  4. Press Ctrl+A to select the coordinate string.
  5. Press Ctrl+C to copy it.
  6. Press Esc to cancel out of chat rather than sending the message.
  7. Alt-Tab to the WarDogs Range Calculator and press Ctrl+V in the appropriate coordinate box.

Then repeat the process for the target position.
